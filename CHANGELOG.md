A source in one channel is up to 3.01 dB louder at the centre than it was (0 dB each side, from -3.01 dB) wherever a pan stage was fed one channel: a strip whose nodes are made, a strip with no nodes that plays into a group (the group's own pan took the 3 dB), a rack's chain and the utility. Whatever is after the pan hears the same rise: a post-fader send, a meter's tap, and a group's or the master's compressor, limiter or ducker, whose gain reduction moves with it.
