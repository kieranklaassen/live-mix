- [#32](https://github.com/kieranklaassen/live-mix/pull/32) [`d45a82a`](https://github.com/kieranklaassen/live-mix/commit/d45a82af8357014588ce8e24b334195df04c8409) Thanks [@kieranklaassen](https://github.com/kieranklaassen)! - Score document, operations, undo/redo, operation log and renderer (U28). The score (`Score`, `format: 1`) is a declarative, seconds-first JSON document — tracks (audio, live, instrument), groups, returns, strips with inserts and sends, clips, automation lanes, modulators and routes, master settings, transport loop — that is the source of truth for arrangement, automation and device graphs. `createScore`, `validateScore` (optionally against the device registry), `parseScore`/`serializeScore` (stable output), `migrateScore`.
