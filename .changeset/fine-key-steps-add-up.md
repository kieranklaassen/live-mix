Shift with an arrow key on a knob whose value the host holds (every knob of a
device on a plate) adds up: ten fine steps are one whole step. Each fine press
started again from the value shown, which is the host's rounded to whole steps,
so the knob moved a tenth of a step once and then stayed, however often the
key was pressed. Knobs that keep their own value were right already.
