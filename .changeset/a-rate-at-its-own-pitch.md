A rate at the pitch the sound was recorded at: `EngineOptions.keepPitch` takes the `signalsmith-stretch` factory, and `engine.preparePitch()` makes one `PitchKeeper` per audio track, which each track's voices are summed into ahead of its strip while the transport runs at another rate, shifted back by `−12·log2(rate)` semitones. Out of the path at rate 1. `IoLatency.keptSec` is what the keepers hold the sound back by (0 at rate 1), and `ChannelStrip.keepPitch(node)` sums a strip's sources into a node ahead of it. Never heard in a browser.
