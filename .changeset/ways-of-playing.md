Ways of playing a sound (`./dsp`): twenty named rules in six families (`WAYS`, `WAY_FAMILIES`) that write the notes of a few takes of a sound from the sound's own notes, its key, a seed and one amount, `far`. Where a variant is the sound played once more, differently, a way gives two to eight takes that differ from one another, to be played in turn. `waysFor(sound)` lists the ways with something to play on a sound without rendering, `wayTakes(sound, { way, seed, far })` says how many takes there are and the round the way plays them in (or why there are none), `playSound(sound, { way, seed, far, take })` gives a take's recipe, `renderFactorySound` and `renderGeneratedSound` take it as `plays`, and `describeTake` says what a take does. A take keeps the sound's instrument, effects, length and loop, stays in its key, leaves every moment that holds the note the sound stands on as written, and plays its hardest note as hard as the sound's hardest. A way that gives fewer than two different takes on a sound has nothing to play there. `plays` and `vary` go together (`playAndVarySound`): the way writes the notes and the variant gives its chord and its touch, with every take of a way on the one chord the variant puts the sound on; a variant's speed and pattern are not read where a way has takes. `takeOrder(order, takes, { length, seed, holds })` lists take numbers for a stretch of passes in one of four orders (`TAKE_ORDERS`: in turn, dealt, mostly stays, grows), for a host to name in a clip's turns. Without `plays` every render is what it was. The table is in `docs/ways-generated.md`. Bench: `FACTORY_REPORT=ways`.
