Session grid (U31, R8): scenes × clip slots over the score with quantised launch and follow actions. Score `format: 3` adds `scenes`, `slots` (one per audio track × scene: a `SlotClip` or a stop, `quantize?`, `launchMode` `trigger | gate | toggle`, `legato`, `follow?`) and `transport.quantize`; format-2 documents migrate (`2 → 3`: empty lists, `'bar'`; format 1 runs `1 → 2` first). New operations `transport.quantize`, `scene.add/remove/move/rename`, `slot.add/remove/update` (a scene takes its slots with it, a track its slots, a source in use by a slot cannot be removed); `normaliseClip` now keeps `warp`/`semitones`. `Session({ document, engine?, tempo?, random?, … })` performs the grid through operations: a launch is `clip.add` on the track's arrangement lane at the next grid line (`'none' | 'bar' | 'beat' | n bars | { seconds }` on the document's tempo map), a stop is `clip.trim` at the boundary plus a fade of the live voice, a scene launch or stop-all is one `batch`/undo step; one clip plays per track, legato carries the position across, gate releases and toggles stop on the grid. Follow actions (`none | stop | again | previous | next | first | last | any | other`, A/B with a probability and a follow time in bars or seconds) are evaluated on the scheduler tick from an injected random source. `quantizeLaunch`, `followTimeSeconds`, `drawFollowAction`, `resolveFollowAction`, slot/scene lookups and defaults are exported; `Scheduler.onTick(listener)` reports every pass with its `reason` (additive; `tick()`/`refresh()` gain an optional reason). `./react` gains `useSession` and `useSlot`. Docs in `docs/session.md`.
