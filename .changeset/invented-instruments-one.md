Four instruments of ideas no one instrument has, each with sixteen presets of its own and twenty in the bank. `flock`: every key calls a handful of small voices that fly in from elsewhere in pitch and space, gather on the note and hover. `magnet-piano`: piano strings sung by magnets tuned to one harmonic of the string, so a note swells out of nothing and can be swept up its own overtones, with a felt hammer to taste. `overtone`: a throat, pipe or reed drone whose mouth picks single harmonics out of its own tone and walks them by itself. `staircase`: stacked octaves under a fixed bell of loudness that glide, step in semitones or walk the held chord, always rising or falling and never arriving.
