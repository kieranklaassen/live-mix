Twelve instruments of ideas no one instrument has, each with sixteen presets of its own and twenty in the bank. `flock`: every key calls a handful of small voices that fly in from elsewhere in pitch and space, gather on the note and hover. `magnet-piano`: piano strings sung by magnets tuned to one harmonic of the string, so a note swells out of nothing and can be swept up its own overtones, with a felt hammer to taste. `overtone`: a throat, pipe or reed drone whose mouth picks single harmonics out of its own tone and walks them by itself. `staircase`: stacked octaves under a fixed bell of loudness that glide, step in semitones or walk the held chord, always rising or falling and never arriving. `shortwave`: every key a far station in static, a whistle, a warble, pips or a murmur that slides in to its note, fades and drifts. `ice`: a frozen lake tuned to the keys, where a strike far off arrives as a falling chirp, echoes off the shores and the ice cracks by itself under held keys. `rewind`: notes played backwards that swell into their strike, after a set time or when the key is let go. `droplets`: the chord held is rained on, from an even pulse to loose rain, onto glass, wood, metal, water or felt. `prepared-piano`: a piano with a bolt, rubber, felt or paper on its strings, and a Mixed setting where every key has its own object. `graft`: breath, a bow, a mallet or a pluck set on a string, a stopped pipe, a bar or a bowl, sixteen crossings from two choices. `wind-harp`: the keys held are strings and the wind plays them, its speed lighting one harmonic of each string after another. `afterglow`: a felt, bell, pluck or glass strike whose own partials are caught and raised into a pad that blooms after the strike and fades on its own time.
