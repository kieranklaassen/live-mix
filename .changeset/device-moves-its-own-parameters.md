An LFO on a WASM device's knob is worked out on the audio thread. `WasmDevice.modulate(name, { routes })` hands the stock worklet a parameter's travel, its base and the numbers of what moves it (`lfo` and seeded `random` sources); the worklet sets the parameter once a block from the block's own place on the audio clock, so the swing is smooth live and is there, the same every time, in an offline render, where the matrix's 40 ms `setParam` never moved a WASM device at all. `getParam` stays the knob's set value and `paramAt(name)` is where the value is now, from the same formula (`modulatedParamValue`). `ScoreRenderer` hands a score's `lfo` and `random` routes on such a device to it instead of the `ModMatrix`; the score's format is unchanged. Modulation of any device parameter now goes along the knob's travel (octaves on a log parameter, whole steps on a stepped one), in the matrix too. `Knob` takes `modulation` and draws the swing and the moving value outside React; `DevicePlate`, `DevicePanel` and `DeviceChainView` take `knobHint`; a device can be made already moving (`modulations` in the create options, so an offline render has it from its first block with no message to wait for), and `scoreDeviceModulations` gives a host that renders by hand what to make each device with.
