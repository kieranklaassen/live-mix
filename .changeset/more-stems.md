`@kieranklaassen/live-mix/separation` takes a track apart into as many stems as a separator names, and every call written against four stems and a remainder compiles and behaves as it did, with the same samples from its default run. `Separator`, `SegmentModel`, `Separation`, `SeparationTracks` and `HybridModelSpec` take the stems' names as a type parameter that defaults to the four; a separator and a model say theirs in an optional `stems`, a `Separation` carries them in `names`, and `stemNames` and `partNames` read a separation's order. A name is a lower-case letter followed by lower-case letters, digits or hyphens, and never `remainder`. `joinSeparators([{ separator, take }])` makes one separator of several, run one after another, each kept for the stems the set takes of it. `splitPart(separation, name, separator, { rest })` answers a new separation in which one part is replaced by a second separator's stems and by what they leave of it, which keeps the part's name unless `rest` gives another; the remainder is not touched, and `report.splits` says what each split measured. `createSegmentSeparator(model, { shifts, seed })` can go over a track more than once, each later pass with up to half a second of silence in front drawn from the seed, and pools the passes; one pass, the default, is the run as it was. `createHybridModel` takes a spec of any stems, and two kinds of model join it: `createWaveformModel` for an export that takes a segment's sound and answers every stem's sound, and `createSpectrogramModel` for one that takes a segment's spectrogram and answers one stem's, with its transform at a size of a power of two times 1, 3, 5 or 15. A `SegmentModel` may say `standardize: false`, a `Separator` may say its `steps(frames)`, and progress is one count whose `total` does not change through the passes of a run and the separators of a joined set. `separationTracks` lays any number of parts, the stems in the separation's order and the remainder last, and counts the score's `master` among the ids that are taken. Three refusals a host of the four can meet: an answer that holds a stem its separator did not name is refused by that stem's name, where the stem was left in the remainder without a word; a part laid on a score at another length or channel count than the remainder is refused by the part's name, where it was laid; and a hybrid spec that names a stem twice is refused by that stem's name, where the refusal named the stem the spec lacked. Also the types `JoinMember`, `JoinedName`, `SplitOptions`, `SplitReport`, `WaveformModelSpec` and `SpectrogramModelSpec`. Checked on stand-ins in Node and, for the bounce of seven and eleven parts, in headless Chromium; with real model files in Node only, on a 10.9 s recording and on it repeated to 35.6 s: the default run the same samples as before, three passes, four one-stem files joined, and a split of the vocals. The waveform kind has run on stand-ins only. Nobody has listened to a stem (docs/separation.md).
