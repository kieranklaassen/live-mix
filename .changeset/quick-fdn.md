Forty devices are built with the vector instructions and take less time for the same sound, to the last bit. The delay network reverb takes a little over half the time it took, the bloom reverb, the spectral drifter, the tine piano and the binaural device under half to about half, the prepared piano a third less, and the spectral devices a sixth to a third less, since the kit's Fourier transform works four bins at a time. `"vector": true` in a `device.json` builds a device this way (`docs/recipes/adding-a-spec-device.md`).
