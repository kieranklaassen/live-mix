Fifty-odd devices take less time for the same sound, to the last bit. Forty are built with the vector instructions: the bloom reverb, the spectral drifter, the tine piano, the modal bells, the delay network reverb and the binaural device take about half the time they took or less, the prepared piano and the drone about a third less, and the spectral devices a sixth to a third less, since the kit's Fourier transform works four bins at a time there. The oversampler that the tape, the saturator and others share reads its past in a row. `"vector": true` in a `device.json` builds a device this way (`docs/recipes/adding-a-spec-device.md`).
