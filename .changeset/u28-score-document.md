Score document, operations, undo/redo, operation log and renderer (U28). The score (`Score`, `format: 1`) is a declarative, seconds-first JSON document — tracks (audio, live, instrument), groups, returns, strips with inserts and sends, clips, automation lanes, modulators and routes, master settings, transport loop — that is the source of truth for arrangement, automation and device graphs. `createScore`, `validateScore` (optionally against the device registry), `parseScore`/`serializeScore` (stable output), `migrateScore`. Every edit is a typed, JSON-serialisable `Operation` (46 types: track/group/return/strip/clip/device/send/lane/modulator/route/transport ops plus `batch`) with pure `apply(score, op)` and `invert(score, op)`; removals cascade and their inverses restore every piece at its index. `OperationLog` is append-only with authors, times and checkpoints; `History` gives undo/redo with one step per continuous gesture (same-key operations from one author coalesce within an explicit gesture id or a 500 ms window); `ScoreDocument` ties them together. `ScoreRenderer` / `loadScore(engine, document)` makes an engine follow the document through its public API — same graph, node order and AudioParam events as hand-driving it (golden test) — with live-input audio left to the app. Engine gains additive `onDispose`, `removeLiveInputTrack`/`liveInputs`, `removeReturnTrack`/`returnTracks`, `removeInstrumentTrack`/`instruments`. Docs in `docs/score.md`.
