Five devices: three instruments and two effects. Each is a spec device on `cpp/kit` with a native test that measures what it claims, twenty-four presets of its own, a description of every control, and a plate of its own colour with a display that draws the device's own law. The bank gains twenty-four presets for each instrument, ten chains for Silent Keys and eleven for Buzzing Bridge, which makes 1,408 presets and 385 chains.

Instruments:

- `tierce-bells` (Tierce Bells): cast bells whose third is a control. It can stand anywhere from narrow minor to a fourth, follow the key of the music, or glide from one third to another inside a single strike.
- `wheel-and-dog` (Wheel and Dog): a wheel that bows two melody strings and three drones without end, with a loose bridge that barks when a push of the wheel is sharper than its threshold.
- `hand-horn` (Hand Horn): a natural horn with a crook and a hand in the bell. The notes the tube gives are open; every other key is stopped by the hand, darker the further it moved.

Effects:

- `silent-keys` (Silent Keys): strings under a chord that is held down without a sound, or a walk of chords. Whatever plays wakes only the partials it shares with them. Its roots and keys move with the bank's key.
- `buzzing-bridge` (Buzzing Bridge): the buzz of a curved bridge, a membrane, loose shells, leather or paper, set off by the level and the pitch of the sound and climbing through its partials as it dies away.

Nothing has been listened to: docs/devices.md says for each device what measurement found weak.
