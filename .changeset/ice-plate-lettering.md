A skin can keep its finish off the lettering (`clearLettering`): the display
and the names under the knobs then stand on plain plate, which runs out softly
into the finish around them, and the plate carries `data-lettering="clear"`.
The Ice plate has it: its speckle, as dark as its ink on so pale a plate, ran
through the knobs' names and the words of the display. No other plate changes.
