A second hundred of visual styles, two hundred and forty-one in all, each with two variations.

`VISUAL_STYLES` has a hundred more entries in the groups there were. The styles there were keep their ids, their pictures, their presets and their order, and a new style stands after the older ones of its group. Each new style reads own numbers (`world.own`, up to six of them), and its sixth header line says what each does; it draws as designed where all of them are 0. The new styles have no presets yet.

A style's shader is still a script of its own that is loaded the first time the style is drawn. A host that lists every style with all its words should expect a longer list: the words of all of them are about 180,000 characters.
