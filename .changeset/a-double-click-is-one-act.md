On the devices of a chain, by the pointer:

- A double click is one act on what its first press was on. The browser sends
  the second press to whatever stands under the pointer by then, and a cell
  that moves away when it is pressed left something else there: a double click
  on a plate's "+N" cell put a knob the open plate drew in its place back on
  its default, one on "−" did the same to a knob of the next plate, and one on
  a cross took the next device out as well. A knob resets only for a double
  click that began on it, and a cell of a chain is pressed by a second press
  only when the first was on it too.
- A move cell pressed again with the pointer held where it is goes on with the
  device it moved. The neighbour slides under the pointer with its own cell in
  the same place, and the next press sent that one the same way, which put the
  first device back: two presses moved nothing.
- The wheel moves a knob that is a list of choices, or has few whole steps, one
  step for a notch. A notch is less of such a knob's travel than a step is,
  and what the wheel had turned was put back on the value shown at every
  render, so a notch at a time turned nothing; only a fast burst got through.
- A time under a tenth of a second is told in milliseconds and a rate under one
  hertz with two decimals. "0.00 s" stood for every attack a compressor has,
  and "0.0 Hz" for the low part of a slow rate's travel.
