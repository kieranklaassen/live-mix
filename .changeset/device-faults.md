Six device faults the factory bench found are fixed in the devices themselves (new `.wasm` for each). `ether-reverb`: a freeze switched on with nothing ringing (the "Frozen" preset, a saved session) waits for a sound and holds its tail, where it used to stay silent. `limiter-1176`: a ceiling after the compressor stops what the attack lets through at 0 dBFS (a click left at +18 dBFS with 24 dB of drive). `saturator`: DC Block works under the curve's ceiling, so a clipped low note no longer leaves up to 3.7 dB over full scale; aliasing figures are unchanged. `ember`: the pulse is centred on zero at every width (no DC off centre). `grain-synth`: the built-in sound folds to mono at every position. `zita-rev1`: the dry/wet balance is levelled, so the output no longer falls up to 7 dB as `mix` rises; saved `mix` values keep their balance and come out louder. The nineteen factory presets that end in `zita-rev1` are trimmed by what they gained and sit where they were.
