Three node devices do what their settings say, and a bypass no longer moves a sound in time. A cut's Q reaches the browser's filter in decibels, which is what a low-pass or a high-pass reads it in: the parametric EQ's low and high cut and the delay's damping are flat (Butterworth, 3.01 dB down at the corner and never over flat, where they stood 1.7 dB proud just past it), and the `filter` device's `q` is one number for every shape (0.707 a flat cut, 1 level at the corner, 2 a peak of 6 dB), so its "High-pass rumble" loses its bump and "Low-pass gentle" is 6 dB down at its corner (`cutQDb`, `FLAT_CUT_Q_DB`; a lane on `q` still ramps, through the new `ParamScale`). The `compressor` cancels the make-up gain a `DynamicsCompressorNode` adds by itself (`compressorNodeMakeupDb`), so `makeupDb` 0 means none: under the threshold a sound leaves as loud as it came, and the default device is 3.66 dB quieter than it was. Its presets carry what they were getting and sound as before: Gentle 2 → 5.51 dB, Voice 4 → 11.85, Glue 1 → 2.35, Limit 0 → 3.42; a compressor saved with its own numbers needs the same added (3.66 dB at the defaults). It reports the samples the node looks ahead (264 at 44.1 kHz, not 265), in seconds as well. A bypassed device plays its dry signal as late as it reports: a node device, a WAM plug-in and a rack hold it in a delay, a WASM device in its processor, so a bypassed compressor is no longer 288 samples early and a rack's `mix` under 1 no longer combs. `compressor` and `filter` are version 2, and the dynamics display's `nodeMakeupDb` is gone.
