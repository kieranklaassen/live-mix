A visual can be drawn in a look that is no preset's. `newLook({ kind, base })` makes one by chance: a variation of a preset, of another look or of the style as it draws itself, bred from the style's presets and looked at before it is handed over. A visual carries a look by value (`world`, `called`), `visualLook` reads the one it is drawn in, and `addVisual` and `setVisualPreset` take one where they take a preset (docs/visuals.md). Frames carry its world, so a second window draws it too.
