Six voice cleanup devices in a new `repair` group, among the stock devices `registerStockWasmDevices` registers (`CLEANUP_DESCRIPTORS`): `de-hum` (the mains hum and its harmonics, 50 or 60 Hz), `de-click` (mouth clicks drawn over by linear prediction; holds the sound about 6.5 ms), `de-plosive` (the lows of a pop, only while it lasts), `de-noise` (a steady noise learned as it goes by minimum statistics and turned down with a decision-directed Wiener gain; holds the sound one 21 ms frame), `de-reverb` (a room's echo and tail by spectral subtraction of the late reverberation; one frame) and `de-ess` (the hiss of an "s", judged against the voice so it works at any level). AudioWorklet devices in TypeScript: one bundled processor file (`dist/worklets/cleanup.js`, `loadCleanupProcessor`, `createWorkletCleanup`, `cleanupProcessorUrl`) and one host class (`WorkletCleanup`) for all six; a click-free bypass to the dry sound held as long as the device holds its own; one meter, `reduction`, the dB taken off lately. The kernels (`createCleanupKernel`, `CleanupKernel`) render offline for tests. Defaults are gentle and suit a speaking voice; each has a few presets. The device category list gains `repair` ("Repair"), after Dynamics.
