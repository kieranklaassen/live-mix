`Knob` and everything else on `useParamControl`: a control of **two places** (`wholeSteps` with one step for all of its travel: an Off and On parameter of a device, so every such knob of `DevicePanel` and `DevicePlate`) is switched by a **press**. A press let go within 4 px of where it went down, by mouse, pen or finger, puts it on its other place inside the gesture the press began (`onChangeStart`, one `onChange`, `onChangeEnd`), so a host's undo takes it back in one step. A drag, the wheel and the arrow keys turn it as before. **Enter** with the keys on it switches it too, at the click the browser makes of the key, so a host that keeps clicks from a control keeps this one; Space is still the page's. A double-click on such a control is two presses and no longer resets it (Delete does); the second press of a double click that began on something else, which moved away, is left alone as a double click there is. Controls of three places or more, continuous ones and `Fader` are unchanged. The info view's hint line for such a knob says "A press switches it, and so does Enter with the keys on it. …". New exports: `hasTwoPlaces`, and `twoPlaces` on `controlGestureInfo`'s options; `ParamControlHandlers` gains `onClick`.
