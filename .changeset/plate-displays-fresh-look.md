Plate displays, a fresh look by reviewers who did not build them.

- **The wheel over a display's point** goes by the size of the turn (a notch of 100 px is one step, as before; a trackpad's small events add up to the same), is one undo step for the whole gesture, and leaves a swipe that goes more across than up or down to the chain.
- **`ParamSpec.step`** (new, optional): `1` for a count or whole semitones. A parameter is a list of choices only when it has `choices`, or `step: 1` and no unit; the guess from a whole-number range is gone, so the eleven knobs that run -1 to 1 (and the vowel) set and show what is between. `device.json` takes `"step": 1`; `Filter.type` and the Spectral Drifter's five lists carry their `choices`.
- **A finger**: a swipe that starts on a display beside its points scrolls the chain (`touch-action: none` is off the canvas; a press on a point is kept by a `touchstart` listener), a point at the display's edge is taken where it stands, two quick nudges are not a double press, and a finger's first 4 px on a knob are held back so a swipe the browser takes turns nothing and leaves no undo step. A pointer the browser cancels puts a control back where it was taken.
- **Two points one on the other**: the one moved last is the one taken.
- **Compressor, Ambient Compressor, Ambient Limiter, Swell, Sidechain Ducker**: the line is the level coming out, the figure what the reduction comes to in what is heard (Mix and the make-up counted), a bypassed plate forgets its past, the Ducker says "no key" when nothing keys it, the Swell stays shut in silence.
- **Parametric EQ** draws its curve through each band's own frequency; **Lattice, Spectral Drifter, Octaves, Pitch Shifter** show what the device does (a root keeps its row, the drifter keeps its age, voices rise with Attack, the shift's sign follows Mode).
- A knob's value in hand is set whole on its patch instead of being cut at the knob's width.
- **A display that stands still** listens every 100 ms over the last 150 ms (it was the newest 43 ms every 200 ms), so a short sound between two listenings sets it running. `DisplayTaps.listen(floor, seconds)` is the call; the analyser keeps the longer stretch while the display stands and a reading puts the window back.
- **Reverbs**: a time is written one way everywhere ("2.0 s", "900 ms", to the tenth under ten seconds), the six tail displays light only what Mix lets out and run on for their span after silence (`live.settle`), a figure at the foot of a room's tail stands on a patch, the Vowel's letter stands clear of its handle, a tail's end on the right edge is drawn out by a hand that goes on past it, and the Ether's Decay has no unit (it is an amount on top of Size; the display writes the time). The Convolver's accent is a gold that can be told from its ink.
- **Tremolo**: Vibrato with Shape Random draws the pitch, Vibrato under 1 Hz bends as little as the device does, Harmonic sets the right side apart by Stereo Phase, and the point stays in reach under half the Mix. **Rotary** gives each rotor the level Balance gives it. **Stereo Detune** says the Detune that is set while the device sleeps. **Delay, Analog Delay, Tape Echo, Echo Memory** draw their repeats as loud as Mix lets them out, and a Time point in hand goes on past the right edge to the knob's end.
- **Wear and texture**: the Radio's static is off once nothing sounds, the Vinyl draws what the record adds as loud as Mix leaves it, and Cascade, Glitch, Grain Cloud, Pad Follower, Spectral Blur, Sustainer and Low Bitrate draw the device at work only while Mix lets it be heard. A point whose setting lies past the picture's edge is taken where it is drawn and moves from where the setting lies, without a jump, to both ends of its range (Patina, Vintage Digital's Bits, Tape's Tone, the noise floors). The Grain Cloud's Pitch point sets whole semitones, the Cascade's words say the repeats that are played, Vintage Digital's grain follows Drive's make-up, and the Grain Cloud and the two hold displays end at rest after the sound stops.
- **`DisplayHandle.drag(x, y, hold?)`**: the plate hands every move of one drag the same `DisplayHold`, empty at the press, so a display that has to keep something for the length of a drag keeps it there and two hands on two points each keep their own. The wear displays keep how far past the picture's edge the setting lay in it.

No device's `process` changes.
