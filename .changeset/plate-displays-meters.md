Plate displays, after the third batch.

- Octaves and the Pitch Shifter move with the sound. Octaves draws what the loudest note gets of each voice pale and, inside it, a meter of that voice that rises and falls with the level going in; the Pitch Shifter has a light at 0 and under each voice, as tall as the voice by full scale. Both are still in silence, as every display is.
- The chain's drop marker is edged in the ground's colour, so it reads on a plate whose colour is near the accent's (a cream plate under a teal marker).
- Points to drag on five more displays: Depth on the Tremolo and the Chorus (a ring on the line the swing reaches at its furthest), Spread on the Rotary (the ringed microphone, taken round the horn), Bloom on the Spectral Drifter (the far end of the line the grains drift along), and Delay with Width on the Stereo Detune (the mark across the left copy). Each stops at the exact ends, gives the default on a double press and stands still while the display runs.
- The Tremolo draws Pan the way the device pans: the line rises towards the "L" when the sound goes left. It was drawn upside down.
