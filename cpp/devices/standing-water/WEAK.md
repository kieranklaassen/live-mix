# Standing Water: what is unproven, and what to listen to first

Nobody has heard it; it was judged by measuring.

Listen first to the default chord: the splash, the dip of about 7 dB a fifth of a second later, and the swell. A single held key then wanders over 7 dB. Whether that is water or a hole is for an ear, and so are the beads that fall back (Splash full, Shake nothing).

Squares and Eightfold rest their pitch on a small plain tone and the splash.

- A patch under its threshold with Splash under a seventh is quiet (about -45 dBFS on a chord).
- A Long pool with Squares or Eightfold can be mostly 2 to 5 kHz.
- In Height, Depth prints only 0.3 to 0.5 dB a quarter when Below or foam fills the gaps.
- By default the last quarter of Layers, Unrest, Drift and Below prints about 0.4 dB.
- On Height patches the patterns are 1.7 to 4 dB apart.
- Keys under 60 Hz stop at 6 to 8 kHz.
- Ten keys at full gain come within 0.1 to 0.5 dB of the knee.
