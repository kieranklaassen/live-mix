# Returning Wave: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it. All was measured, by the builder and by a checker.

Listen first to one long low key at the defaults. It opens bright, is a warm
haze after a second, passes its double octave, twelfth and octave and is whole
and bright again at 12 seconds. The return should be heard as an arrival.

Then:

- **Reading**, Flow, is mostly breath. By pitch, or with a slow Return in a
  ring, it can be very quiet.
- **Two rooms** adds an octave under the key. Is it wanted in a chord?
- **Bowl**: Wave and Density are near each other (under 2 dB). **Fading
  well**: Wave and Flow are.
- **Gap** is silent with no Second, and both are small colours under a half.
- **Leak** above a half ends on the anchor alone, a plain tone.
- **Anchor** at zero lets the pitch go for seconds.
- **Blur**, **Haze** and **Splash** do little in their first quarter.
- **Tilt**, **Body** and **Throw** all at the top are hard on the ear.
- A shared clock with a short Return pumps all the keys as one.
