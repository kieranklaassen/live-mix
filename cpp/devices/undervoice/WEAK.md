# What is unproven in Undervoice

Nobody has heard it. Everything here was voiced by measurement.

Listen first to:

1. The default chord. The door to the octave below should read as dark voices
   that open and shut, and not as a level that wobbles.
2. Whispers on bright vowels (Whisper over 0.7, Vowel over 0.7) with Eight or
   Three: mostly 2 to 5 kHz, so hissy.
3. Drips. Each key counts its own power of two, so the drops of a chord fall at
   unlike rates.
4. Shared. The fit is a compromise for tempered chords: the purr is common to a
   few parts in a hundred, so it beats slowly.
5. Low close chords. Three keys a tone apart under A2 lose most of their floor.

Each Division carries its own stance of throat, chest, lips and width, so the
same Vowel and Throat do not say quite the same in all of them.

Weak ends: Second division from five to eight, Pace's fast end, Attack under
40 ms, Chest with Drips, Turn when both divisions are One. Throat bells' high
keys are 8 dB down. The nearest divisions are 2.8 dB apart at the defaults.
