# Closed Eyes: what is unproven

Nobody has listened. Every claim is a measurement.

Listen first to one slow chord at the defaults, held four seconds and let go:
does the opposite read as the light behind the note, or as another note that
enters? Then play the chord again at once: it comes back paler with its
opposite in it. Then the same with each Opposite.

- Sides trembles and Breath comes in waves, the same for every key: perhaps
  too much.
- Mirror throws low keys high and fades in about half of After Length.
- Edge near the top with Breath, or Rough after a bright vowel, is the
  brightest corner.
- Lamp and Reed, Reed and Vowel, Mirror and Sides are just under 4 dB apart;
  Struck and String 2 to 3 dB on short notes.
- Recover and Safe show only when other keys follow. Depth, Form and Colour
  are small under a loud opposite.
- A guard turns loud sums down; its return is unheard.
- Depth at the top with Chord memory brings a tired chord back very faint.
- Without the vector instructions it costs about three times as much.
