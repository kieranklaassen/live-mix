# What is unproven

Nobody has heard it. It was voiced by measurement.

- Listen first to a slow chord at the defaults, then move Fold, Curl and Turn. Curl uses a lagging radius so that it differs from Fold; whether it sounds like vowels is not known.
- Listen to Broken next. Its clouds take a whole key down for a second at a time, which may be too much for a pad. Fold, Curl and Decay do little under it.
- About one random patch in nine is still poor: rings past a dark Light with a short Decay are quiet, and Pointed with Light near the top is thin and bright.
- Tilt, neighbouring Petals and the first quarters of Bloom, Attack and Decay move little (0.2 to 0.5 dB).
- In a dark folded bass, Smooth and Pointed, and the first two orders, come within 2 dB of each other.
- Many hard keys are ridden down just under the clip. A very sharp strike can pass it for a moment.
- The heart comes forward where Light hides every ring.
- Above about A5 Fold and Curl do less.
