# What is unproven in Night Drum

Nobody has heard it. It was judged by figures.

Listen to first:

- The default chord, held and let go. Is the roll a warm tone or a patter?
- Single strokes on D2 and D5 with Finger, Thumb, Palm and Stick.
- Ten loud keys. Many drums at once stand several dB back (`make_room`),
  which must never be heard as pumping.
- Stick on Shallow with a roll, the brightest patch.
- Hum on Kettle beside Hum on Pot.

Known limits:

- Off Centre at its default leaves overtones 3, 5 and 7 up to 15 cents from
  the harmonic. The note itself is within half a cent.
- Grain on single long strokes is only the first 50 ms. Bend and Fall under
  Hum are only the start of the note.
- Of 120 random patches about one in ten is outside: harsh sticks, or ten keys
  at full gain a little over the knee (Stick on None or Tube, high Grain).
- A noisy hand (Brush) strays by several cents on a short ring.
- Without the vector flag the cost is twice as high.
