# Flame Organ: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it. Everything was measured, nothing judged by ear.

Listen first to:

- The default chord for ten seconds: whether the notes that lift by themselves sound like a gift or like a mistake. Lift is the knob.
- Flared with Spirit and much Roar: the brightest corner, most of it over 2 kHz. A low key struck hard there can peak over -6 dBFS.
- Lag or Place turned fast on Flared with Spirit: the new harmonic overshoots for a fifth of a second.
- Between from 0.3 to 0.6 with Second Flame up: the slip before the beat.

Known limits: only the first five harmonics may take the note, so the high corners of Lag and Place colour it more than they move it. Coal with much Roar hides Lag and Place. With a short Catch and much Glass, Open and Double are close. A flame with fuel for a second or two can be gone before a slow Catch has let it sing. The levels of the twenty pairs of tube and fuel are a fit, and a far corner misses it by 4 to 8 dB.
