Tierce Bells: nobody has heard it, every claim is a figure. The figures that worry most are about what makes a strike sound like a bell. Every key has the same level, the same decay and the same mixture of partials from C1 to C8, so a C7 rings as long as a C2 (Decay says how long, as the spec asked) and nothing thins or shortens up the keyboard; a small real bell does both. The strike is bright and clean: a hard clapper is heard as a burst of filtered noise 17 dB under the strike at Hardness 1 (its level over the first 10 ms, 25 dB under at the default 0.5), set by measurement only, and whether it reads as iron on bronze or as a hiss is for an ear. Struck again on a ringing bell the new strike goes in a quarter turn from what rings, so it adds as power at every pitch (1.44 times, 3.2 dB, for two equal strikes at 0.6), and a bell may hold up to 1.12 of a full strike a partial; two strikes at full gain end 1 dB up and not 3, and a roll at full strength does not build. A strike with Lean on a bell whose third has come to rest takes the ringing third along to the new place in 0.12 s: it does not jump and no loudness is lost, but it is a short slide (110 cents in an eighth of a second) that a listener may or may not notice. The spec's Glide goes to 0 and this one starts at 0.02 s. Follow on Key leaves the Tierce knob without effect on every note the scale answers, and at the default patch that is every white key.

Room under the clip. Twelve keys at full gain struck once stay under the knee on every own preset (peaks of 0.34 to 0.49 against 0.5, the worst of four chords in four registers), and eight keys at gain 0.8 at 0.21 to 0.40. Struck four times in a row without stopping the same chords pass it on 15 of the 24, to 0.74 on "Major bell" and on "Carillon in D minor" (3.4 dB over; the clip there takes 0.1 to 0.2 dB off the peak), because what was arranged to miss in phase in the first strike no longer does. Strikes piled on one key reach at most 3.4 dB over one strike, with Lean on and the gaps as short as 4 ms. Listen first, on headphones, to "Four to three" with one key held for ten seconds, for the third moving 4/3 to 5/4 inside the one strike; then strike the same key again after six seconds, for the slide; then repeat a low key at full strength on "Cracked tower", for the clapper's noise.
