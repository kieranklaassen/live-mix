# Climbing Gong: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it: everything below was measured.

Listen first to:

- The default chord held for five seconds: does the climb read as one slow
  opening, or as two sounds on top of each other?
- Fall back over a half, on a low key with a roll: its waves may be too even.
- Ten keys with a roll: the shared level leans back within a millisecond of a
  loud stroke, which could be heard as the first keys sinking.
- Wood and yarn on Golden and Plate, where the stroke itself lights the rungs.

Weak in the numbers:

- The nearest choices at the default: felt and brush, felt and yarn, bow and
  brush at just over 3 dB of print distance.
- Under a full roll with a full climb (Rolling wall) felt, yarn and wood are
  under 1 dB apart, and Hardness, Place and Threshold move little.
- Roll speed moves little while Roll is low.
- Choke changes little in its last quarter.
- A very short Low ring (under 2 s) is 8 dB quieter than a long one.
- The brush is 4 dB quieter.
