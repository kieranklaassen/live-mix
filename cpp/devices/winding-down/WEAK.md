Winding Down. Unproven: that the pulse is heard to slow, or to quicken, without end. The layers, their rates, their weights and the wrap follow the law within 0.000007 at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz, and the wrap adds no step of its own, but whether an ear follows one pulse slowing or only hears layers come and go is for a listener. The figures that worry most: with two layers the level swells 1.2 dB once in every halving (0.01 dB or less with three to five); at Rate 8 with five layers 15 % of the pulse weight (30 % at the most) is above 16 Hz, the quickest layer near 38 Hz, which is a flutter on top of the slow pulses; noise comes out 4.3 dB under its input at the defaults and nothing is added back; a Halving of 120 s moves a twentieth of an octave in six seconds, so a short listen shows almost none of it. Listen first to "Two crossing" over two whole halvings for the swell, then to "Eight, woven" for the flutter, then to "Two minute slide" for some minutes to hear whether the slide is felt at all.
