# What is unproven

Everything here was measured, and nobody has heard it.

Listen to these first:

1. Turning bells, slow playing. Is a turn heard as a turn? The brightness at
   each landing may be too much with Strike up.
2. Hovering glass pad with one key held for a minute. The pace falls to an
   eighth at the neck and a small wandering goes on. Does it feel alive?
3. Neck at Pass on each body. The other side is another weight of the same
   partials, so its colour was chosen on paper.

Doubts:

- The choices of Neck are 3.9 dB apart or more on a held chord, and 2.2 to
  4.2 dB on short overlapping keys, which seldom live to meet the neck.
- Bell against Skin is 2.8 dB on short keys.
- A walk is brought to one loudness by a sum at eight places. Of 120 random
  patches three are faint: a deep Depth with Split below zero, or with a
  slow Attack on String.
- Settling and the first steps of Attack and Grains are small in the print.
- Low Pour with a high Depth is a pluck: its beginning is 5 dB fainter in the
  loudest 400 ms than the default.
- The bodies are sums of sines. They may sound plain with Beat, Exchange and
  Grains all at zero.
