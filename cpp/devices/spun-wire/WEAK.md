# What is unproven in Spun Wire

No person has heard it. All below was measured.

- **The seam.** Plucks become a rub within 1.8 dB from 4 to 50 passes a second. Listen first to one held key with Spin and Melt at 12, then Spin at 25.
- **The coast.** Let the default chord go: its last five plucks should slow and soften together without stopping dead.
- **Release** damps the wires from the moment the key is up. Hear it at 0.1 and at 5.
- **Pluckers.** Nail and Air are 3.1 dB of print apart at the defaults, Feather and Air 3.8. At a short Ring, Nail and Air come within 1.6.
- **Small steps.** A quarter of Beat or Gauge, the last of Melt and Release, and the first of Rise move the print 0.2 to 0.4 dB.
- **Loud corners.** Ten hard keys came within 3.5 dB of full scale in 24 of 120 random patches: Card or Nail with Edge high.
- **Quiet corners.** One turn with a short Ring, a dark Skin or Air can be 15 dB under the defaults.
- **Melt and Coast** do nothing for One turn.
