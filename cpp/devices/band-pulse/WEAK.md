The figure that worries most is the peak: the split turns phase, so even with Depth at 0 a bare square wave fed at -1 dBFS comes out 9.5 dB over its own peak, one saw 7.5 dB and a chord of saws 4 to 5 dB, and the pulse can add 1.8 dB more; nothing trims it (no make-up, no clip, no dry path). The bank's phrase is safe: fed at -1 dBFS the worst presets, Three high ticks and Eight up high, peak 0.46 dB over the input and none passes full scale. So listen first to a bare bright synth fed hot, for clipping after the device. The gap is only as deep as the band is wide: at Depth 1 with Low 200 and High 1000 a tone at the middle of the band falls 22.5 dB, one at Low or High only halves, the band as a whole falls 11 dB and sound half an octave outside it ripples 3 to 4 dB; four octaves are needed for silence at the middle, and Narrow flutter, an octave wide, dips 9 dB. Listen to whether the default reads as a pulse on the middle or as a tremolo on everything near it. A control thrown in the middle of a pulse never jumps, but with Edge under 2.5 ms a thrown Rate, Duty or Offset is 0.16 to 0.23 of the way in 8 samples (the gate's own limit), and 0.32 at Rate 40 with Duty at 0.1 or 0.9, where the edge is held to 1.25 ms: listen for a tick there. A fast pulse also writes new tones beside every partial: at Rate 40 a 220 Hz tone gains one at 180 Hz 16 dB down and sound under 100 Hz 33 dB down, under a Low of 200. Unproven: whether a 2 ms edge on a low note (Hard chop, Forty ticks) is itself heard as a click, and whether the presets above twelve a second are told apart by ear, since the preset tests cannot read those rates.
