# What is unproven in Bent Blade

Nobody has heard it. Everything here was measured.

Listen first to the default chord, bowed and then breathed: is the breath air or hiss when Edge is above three quarters? Then to Twin at Tunnel 0.1 and at 9: a tide, then a shiver.

- Glide Law is a law between keys. At the default Glide, Straight and Nearest are 1 to 1.5 dB apart and From one 2 to 3 dB from both; at 2 s From one is over 4 dB away. It shows in a line and a slow chord.
- At the defaults the wells are 3.0 to 4.2 dB apart, and breath and bow 3 to 4. A mallet on a twin and on a fifth is 2.1 dB apart.
- The print does not hear Concord, Tremble and Wander well (0.2 to 0.6 dB a quarter): they move partials and cents.
- With no second well Far Well and Tunnel do nothing.
- The well at the octave is bass heavy on low keys, even with what lies under 115 Hz turned down.
- A guard turns very loud chords down for about a second; its first 64 samples can pass the knee by half a decibel.
- The header asks for vector instructions itself where the build does not. It is over 1000 lines.
