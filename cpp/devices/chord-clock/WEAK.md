# Chord Clock: what is unproven

Measured, never heard. Listen first to the default chord for ten seconds, then "Octave stair keys", "Breath engine" and "Sevens on the loom".

- Fully drawn ratios move keys up to 17 cents at the default, 35 with sevens, 49 with elevens. Beside a tempered instrument turn Agree down; the pattern then drifts.
- A new lowest key makes held keys glide to new ratios in about 90 ms. Letting the lowest key go moves nothing.
- Keys are each softer the more there are (3 dB on five keys, 7 on ten at full force). It may be heard under a sparse melody over a drone.
- Choices are over 4 dB apart at the defaults, not on every patch: on a plucked glass patch the paths come as near as 2 dB.
- Release under 0.6 s and Touch at one force measure small on a print. On noisy patches Agree, Ratios, Stagger and Lower do little.
- Tone and Rhythm both low is simply quiet (one random patch in ten); skin with both high meets the soft clip.
- Near fusion noisy pulses are 8 dB softer than slow ones.
