Binaural. Nobody has heard it. The signal is proven (each ear's tone sits half of Beat from the key to five decimals at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz, the browser build matches the native one to the sample), but the beat between the ears is made by the listener and no measurement here shows that it is heard, nor that the 5 ms fade of a stolen voice or a jump of Beat under a held chord passes unnoticed. The figure that worries most is the bed: it is added on top of the chord scale, so with Bed at 1 the peaks pass the soft clip's knee (0.15 % of samples with one key at full gain, 0.31 % with eight, peaks to 0.80), where the clip adds tones of its own inside each ear; on the bench's phrase no factory preset reaches the knee (worst peak 7.4 dB under full scale), but that phrase is not a full pool, and with a full pool five own presets pass it (see the last paragraph). Cross is 3.0 dB lower in level than Pair and Warmth at 1 is 3.7 dB lower than Warmth at 0 at the same peak, so two of the three Cross presets sit 3 dB under the middle of the bank. Listen first, on headphones, to one key of "Six a second" and lift one earpiece: the beat should stop. Then "Forty crossed" against "Forty in the air", then hold eight keys and strike a ninth, then play A1 over A2 (two keys on one pitch, which cancelled by up to 54 dB before the fix), then "Dark bed, three" at a hard touch for grit.

A full pool against the knee, measured at 48 kHz with eight keys held at full gain on every own preset and every bank preset. The harness holds a full pool at or under the knee at the default Volume with Bed no higher than 0.3, and does not go through the presets. Five of the six own presets with the bed pass it. "Dark bed, three" is 1.7 dB over on a chord of fourths in 14 s and 2.5 dB over in 120 s, and 4.3 dB over with the eight keys on one pitch, where the clip bends the peak 0.36 dB and what it adds is 56 dB under the sound. "Brown bed, slow" is 0.1 dB over on the chord and 3.0 dB over on one pitch. "Pink bed, six", "Rising to three" and "Ten down to six" are 1.7, 1.3 and 0.5 dB over on one pitch, less on eight octaves of A, and under on the chord. Of the thirty bank presets two pass, "Struck pairs, seven" and "Eight and four", which have no bed and a Volume 2 and 2.5 dB over the default: by 0.4 dB with eight keys on one pitch at full gain, and not at gain 0.8.
