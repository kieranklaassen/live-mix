# Wire Lantern: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it. All of it was measured.

Listen first to:

- The default on the slow chord. Every note carries its own fourth under its octave, which may cloud close chords.
- Bow. No friction is modelled: lines are held at wavering levels and the hair is noise that grips once a period.
- Breath. The solids are 2 to 3 dB apart, the least of the four exciters; Touched Corner, Corner Loss and Pluck Point say least here.
- Cube. Nearly one sine after two seconds with the finger.

Dead ends: the first quarter of Swell (50 to 160 ms), of Contact with the finger, and Sympathy on a web of low True, where the tones miss the wires and the knob mostly turns the note down.

A hard finger or hammer on the prism or web with much Contact puts ten full keys 3 to 5 dB into the clip. A finger resting on a corner all through a slow swell leaves little to hear.

The build without vector instructions, which `dev-device.sh` makes, costs 5.7 to 7.6 percent with eight keys; the built one 2.8 at most.
