Twin Bars: nobody has heard it, every claim is a figure. The figure that worries most is the room law that keeps sixteen keys of a chord under the clip at the default patch. It turns the whole sum down as keys are added, so a held key at gain 0.8 falls 2.3 dB within a millisecond when three more keys land beside it (5.1 dB at full strength) and comes back at 2 to 4 dB per 100 ms as they die, faster with Damp turned up. Above 1 kHz the fall is 52 to 73 dB under the strokes that cause it, so it is not a click, but whether it is heard as pumping only an ear can say, and inside a big chord a key rings longer at first than Decay says. Also unproven by ear: with the hardest mallet and the brightest Tone four wooden keys at gain 0.8 pass the clip's knee (peak 0.68, eight metal keys 0.59), and two presets as shipped do on eight keys (see the last paragraph); a roll below full strength swells 3.5 to 4.3 dB over one stroke where a roll at full strength is held to 1.3 dB; a key struck again keeps its beat only while its low tone is less than 40 dB under the new stroke, after which the beat starts again at its crest; a key rolled faster than Scatter strikes its second bar less often than its first. Listen first, on headphones, to one low key held on Four a second while short four note chords are played above it, for the held key ducking and coming back; then strike one key every half second on Slow metal bowl, for the beat running on through each stroke.

The presets against the clip's knee, measured at 48 kHz on every own preset and every bank preset. The room law counts the low tones only, so the overtones and a preset's Volume are outside it, and the harness holds sixteen keys on three chords at the default Volume and never on one pitch. Sixteen ids on one pitch at full gain are 2.9 dB over the knee at the default patch and pass it on 18 of the 24 own presets. Sixteen keys a semitone apart from C4, also at full gain, pass it on six: "Wood knock" by 3.7 dB, "Flam of wood" 3.5, "Wood flutter" 1.9, "Scattered metal" 1.2, "Eight, hard" 0.5 and "Three, short" 0.3. Only the five own presets whose Volume is over the default were held key by key. In the bank 26 of the thirty presets have Volume over the default, set for the bank's loudness window; all thirty pass on one pitch and 25 with different pitches. The furthest is "Flam on wood": 4.1 dB over on the semitones, where the clip bends the peak 0.31 dB and what it adds is 48 dB under the sound, and 9.3 dB over on one pitch, where the bend is 3.4 dB and what it adds is 30 dB under. It passes from three close keys at full gain and four at gain 0.8. Listen to six or more keys close together at a hard touch on "Wood knock", for grit on the knock.
