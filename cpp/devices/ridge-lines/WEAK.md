# Ridge Lines: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it.

Listen first to the default chord D3 A3 F4 C5 E5 for ten seconds: do the
overtones glide past each other, or do they only shimmer? Then Cliff lead,
Winter bells and High pass train, the brightest.

- Two choices of Near or Far are about 3 dB apart on the print at the least
  (Near: Pines and Cliffs 2.9, Far: Hills and Cliffs 2.8). Part of the distance
  between two of Near is how long the note rings.
- Parallax, Vibrato, Company, and the low half of Attack are movement: they are
  heard more than the print shows (0.1 to 0.6 dB a quarter). Glow shows on
  notes that fall and hardly on held ones.
- With Far on Pines the sound is as wide as it is loud; the fundamental keeps
  the fold to mono.
- Far on Pines with Near on Towers and much Wind is the harshest corner.
- Ten hard keys are turned down by a slow gain, some 6 dB.
- The plate colour given allows white ink at a contrast of 5 to 1, not 6.
