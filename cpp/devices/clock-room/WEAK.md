# Clock Room: what is unproven

Nobody has heard it. Every figure was measured on renders.

Listen first to:

1. The default chord held for ten seconds in oak. The clocks strike apart and fall into step over the first two seconds, then strike as a short roll. The level moves by about 8 dB with the swing. It may be taken for a tremolo.
2. Long wall and Night hall, then Learn turned from zero to full under a held chord.
3. Splayed beside Chain, and Opposed beside In step. Each wall also changes the colour of its clocks.

Doubtful:

- The gongs are voiced tables, not measured metal. True below one half is raw metal and can sour a chord.
- A strike or a rub is brought near one loudness, so a dull strike in the glass dome is raised by up to 18 dB.
- Second rod, True and Glow do little in a dry bright patch: under half a decibel of print distance a quarter there.
- In a nested patch with much Glow, Verge, Chime hammer and Rub only are within 2 to 3 dB of each other.
- Of 120 random patches four were too quiet (a short gong in a dry case) and six put two fifths of their energy over 2.5 kHz.
