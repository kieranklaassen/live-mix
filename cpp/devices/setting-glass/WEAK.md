# What is unproven in Setting Glass

Nobody has heard it. All of it was judged by figures.

- The clearing. Each key starts as breath near its tones and the tones set from
  the top down. Is it glass, or filtered noise over bells? Listen to this first:
  the defaults, D3 A3 F4 C5 E5, held for five seconds, then let go.
- Release on Remelt: the tones fall one by one from the lowest up. Is that
  melting, or a second chord?
- Neighbours shakes held, set keys at each new strike. Too much at the top?
- Blows go on after the key where the strikes fall at the end.
- Order and Stretch move and thin the overtones more than a print shows.
- With Heat at zero, Cooling and Edge do nothing, as nothing melts.
- Set and Press are alike when the glass is cold. Breath is small
  on a bare key that waits for its release.
- Pour on a cold ringing pad is the closest strike to Drop (2 to 3 dB).
- Short Ring with Tap or Rod is quiet: raise Volume.
- Crystal with Bright near the top has real weight over 4 kHz.
- Sugar plucks and Grains on the floor are the quietest presets.

Then listen to Remelting swells, Crystal dawn and Obsidian voices.
