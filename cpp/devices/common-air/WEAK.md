# Common Air: what is unproven

Listen first to the turn of the breath on the opening chord, about two and a half seconds in. The chord falls some ten decibels and the reeds come back one by one. Whether that reads as a breath or as a hole is for an ear.

Then:

- Both and Circular measure 2 dB apart in a short print; they part over several breaths.
- Share, Lock, Beat and Chiff move how a chord behaves more than its spectrum, and measure 0.2 to 0.6 dB a quarter. So does the bottom of Attack.
- One random patch in four is bright or thin over 2 kHz, most of them steel or paper.
- The breath noise of paper and of circular breathing is tuned by measurement only.
- Lock pulls a tempered third fourteen cents to pure. Beside a tempered instrument that sounds out of tune.
- The order in which keys come back is partly drawn anew for each breath, which can hide it.
- The pipes are short loss lines: a pipe does not slow the reed it stands on.
- Nobody has heard it. All figures come from renders.
