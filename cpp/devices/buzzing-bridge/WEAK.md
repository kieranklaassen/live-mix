Buzzing Bridge: nobody has heard it, every claim is a figure. The figure that worries most is attacks, since Loose shell is nothing else. Of 1,344 notes in fast ringing runs made up for it (plucks, dull plucks, bars and soft sine bells, four to eleven a second, half of the runs with every other note at half strength) the shell is knocked by 926 (it was 816 before the check). Plucks and bars at eight a second are nearly all heard, bass plucks mostly, high plucks at eight a second about three in eight, and bells with a soft start at eight a second about two in eight. The other way round, a deep tremolo, vibrato, hiss, beating saws and held chords knock nothing after their start, but a held cluster of three notes a semitone apart, each of three detuned saws, still knocks twice in four seconds (18 dBFS down, 10 dB under the pad). Around the same listening also sits the pitch: on a held detuned chord with two or three possible roots it still leaps about once in four to five seconds (42 times in ten chords before, 9 now), and a note reached with no attack takes a quarter of a second to be believed, so a slur drags the bridge's place a little behind it. A chord with no common period has no pitch at all and the bridge sits where it would for Resonance / 16.

The bridge on a held sound is the second thing. On a pluck the default is plain by any figure (buzz 12 dB under the sound, the dying upper partials 10 to 40 dB over what they were from half a second on), but a saw or a pad that does not die had only a 3 dB rise about its third partial, so now it climbs half of its way over some seconds (kHeldClimb) and the end of the climb is heard throughout (kEndShare): 4 to 6 dB about the fifth partial and 1.5 to 3 dB above 1.5 kHz on a pad. Whether that is a buzz or a colour only an ear can say; a steady bright saw gains under 1 dB up there. Peaks are now held to twice the sound by a gain that catches at once (5.85 dB over at the worst of 11,000 renders, up to 11.2 dB before), which at Amount 1 takes 0.5 to 1.4 dB off the buzz of Membrane, Paper and Loose shell and nothing off the default; that gain cuts the first crest that meets it, which is not measured for what it adds. On a pluck the octave under the bridge's place can sink 2 to 3 dB where the buzz meets the sound's own second partial. Measured at 44.1 to 192 kHz every model is within 1 dB in level, and Grazing at 192 kHz is 1.2 dB brighter above 3 kHz at the attack of a note over a ringing one. Listen first to the default on one plucked note left to ring, then on a held organ-like chord, then to Three shells on fast repeated plucks with soft notes among them.
