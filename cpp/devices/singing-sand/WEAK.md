# What is unproven in Singing Sand

Nobody has listened to it. Every claim is a number from a render.

Listen to these first.

- **Dry** from the middle up, in **Far rain** and **Flint plucks**. The single
  grains are pats of noise, and may sound like a bad line.
- The bottom of **Lockstep**, where a key is mostly its own noise and up to
  8 cents off. It may be too weak to play a tune on.
- **Cluster** at 3 and up. The overtone of the group is well over the note, so
  it may be heard as a new key.
- **Squeak** with **Grain** up and **Bury** down: most of it is at 2 to 5 kHz.
- **Aftershocks** and **Slips** at a fast **Pace** with a steep **Slope** are
  10 dB under the rest, and slow slips can be as much over it.

Of 180 patches drawn at random 13 were too quiet, too bright or over the
clip's knee. **Round** is closest to the other sands (3.5 dB to **Flint**).
**Front**, a short **Run** and the top of **Layer Order** move the default
chord least.
