# Isochronic: what is weak

Nobody has listened to this device; every figure here is a measurement. The figure that worries most comes from the family's level law: the sum is divided by the root of the keys' envelopes squared, so a held key gets louder as the others die. With three of four keys let go it rises 6.0 dB in all, 1.79 dB in the first 100 ms at Release 2 s and 2.3 dB at 0.5 s; with seven of eight let go, 9.0 dB in all and up to 3.9 dB in 100 ms. By the same law a chord of five let go together holds its level for 0.2 s at Release 1.5 s, and for 2 s at Release 15 s, before it falls. Next, Rate thrown at an Edge of 2 ms while a key sounds: the gate catches up along a straight ramp, which puts 24 dB under the tone above 1.5 kHz, 21 to 24 dB more than the pulse's own edge, with no step; whether that is heard as a tick is not known. Next, with Air part way eight keys at full strength pass the clip's knee: a chord on 23 samples in ten minutes, eight keys on one pitch on 1.9 % of samples, and the clip holds them. At the default Volume tone alone and noise alone stay under it. The noise is the same in both ears and 3 dB under the tone. A change of Duty or Edge waits for the next pulse, up to 2.4 s at the slowest Rate. Listen first to a chord let go one key at a time at a short Release, then to Rate turned quickly at Edge 2 ms, then to a full chord with Air near a quarter.

The presets against the knee, measured at 48 kHz with eight keys held at full gain on every own preset and every bank preset. The harness proves the room at the default Volume and does not go through the presets, and presets go past it. Four own presets with Air part way ("Fourteen a second", "Eighteen light", "Ten to six", "Eleven to two") pass by 0.9 to 1.2 dB with the eight keys on one pitch and none on a chord, and so do four bank presets at the default Volume ("Fourteen flutter", "Eighteen light", "Ten down to six" and "Three to the second", by 1.0 to 1.2 dB). Five of the six bank presets whose Volume is raised pass too, four of them with Air at 0. The furthest are the bank's "Eight short keys" and "Forty clicks", 3 and 2.5 dB over the default Volume: 0.8 and 1.3 dB over the knee on a chord in ten seconds ("Eight short keys" 2.3 dB over in 120 s) and 3.0 and 2.5 dB over on one pitch, from five keys at full gain. There the clip bends the peak 0.12 dB at the most and what it adds is 43 dB under the sound. At gain 0.8 the two pass on one pitch only, from six and seven keys.
