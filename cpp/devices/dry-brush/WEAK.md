# What is unproven

Nobody has listened to it.

Listen first to:

- The default chord held for ten seconds. Do the hairs blur into one warm stroke?
- Dryness at the top with little Ink. Is it read as a brush running dry?
- Trembling at high Speed, near to a plain tremolo.
- Reed pen and Feather on Silk with a pale Shade and much Grain: the harshest corner.

Weak by the numbers:

- Soak, Lean and Bleed do little under Dry field. Spread, Grain and the upper half of Pressure do little on short dots, and the quick half of Attack moves the sound least.
- Trembling with Level and Reed pen with Hair come near each other in extreme patches. At the defaults the closest pair of scripts is 3.6 dB apart, of brushes and of papers 4.0.
- About a quarter of random patches go past the knee of the soft clip when ten keys are struck at full velocity at the default Volume.
- The plate's colour allows no ink past 5.4 to 1 in contrast, so the words are white.
