# Coloured Noise: what is weak

Nobody has heard it. Unproven by ear: whether the swells feel unpaced (the same train of swells follows every silence), whether a band on white noise reads as a pitch, and whether anyone notices that an eleventh key waits 50 ms for its voice. The figure that worries most is the level: at the default Volume the loudest 400 ms of the bank's held phrase reads minus 31.3 dBFS, 1.3 dB under the bank's floor, and a bank preset needs Volume near minus 8 dB, where ten low keys at full gain on brown noise at Tint 1 touch the clip's knee twice in ten minutes (1,470 times at minus 6 dB). Also unsettled: Tint makes the whole louder by nothing to 4.4 dB depending on Colour and key, keys a semitone apart share part of one band and were not measured against the knee, and an id sent again at another pitch while a second key holds the bed up reads 0.16 to 0.18 on the click measure (0.04 of the sound's peak in eight samples). Listen first to High cut swept down from its open end on white noise at 44.1 kHz, then to one low key repeated on brown noise at Tint 1 under a long Release, then to an eleventh key struck into ten held ones.

The presets against the knee, measured at 48 kHz with ten keys held at full gain on every own preset and every bank preset: nothing passes. No own preset and none of the thirty bank presets, whose Volume is 2 to 6 dB over the default, puts a sample over 0.5 in 10 to 16 s. Nine patches were held for ten minutes, the default and eight bank presets. The highest is the bank's "Pink chord", which peaks at 0.452, 0.9 dB under the knee, with ten ids on one low pitch, and at 0.432 on ten low keys a major third apart. The other 22 bank presets and the own presets were not held that long, and noise has no fixed peak.
