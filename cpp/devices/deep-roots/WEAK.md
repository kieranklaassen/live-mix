# What is unproven in Deep Roots

Nobody has heard it. Everything was set by measurement.

Listen first:

1. D3 A3 F4 C5 E5 at the defaults. The bass on D2 should grow
   in and feel like the chord's own.
2. The same chord with Meeting far left, in the middle, far right.
3. One high key alone: a bass, or an echo of the key?

Doubts:

- When a finger moves, the bass may slide or swap for half a second.
- Which bass carries a chord is decided by a table of intervals.
- Of 240 random patches 9 were ugly: bright, very quiet, or one too dark.
- The lowest quarter of Attack, Growth, Root tone and Leaf tone on Sine, and
  half of Tolerance, change the chord by less than the half dB asked for.
- Fit and Tolerance do nothing at Meeting 0, and Tolerance little where the
  bass lies on the chord already.
- Peat roots ring about twice as long as Release says.
- Grains and noise are a little brighter at 96 kHz and above.
- Glass, Peat and Chalk lean up to 4 cents off pitch on purpose.
