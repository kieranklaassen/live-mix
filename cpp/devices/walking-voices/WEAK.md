# Weak sides

Nobody has listened to this yet: everything below was measured, not heard.

- Listen first to the default on a slow chord change with one key held through, then to a quick tune. Two people answer each key at once and the rest slide after them, softer. If that smears, lower Reach and Lag.
- Steps and Turn give every note its own pulse, which follows no tempo.
- Agreement, Vibrato, Stubbornness, Reach and Wander are heard and hardly printed: a quarter of each moves the print 0.3 to 1 dB, about what another take does. Attack under 0.2 s is the same.
- Path and Body scale Attack and Release, less so for long times.
- People in close tuning beat slowly: a lone note's level moves some 2 dB, and its lowest partial more.
- With too few people for two a note, People only puts the second of each pair in the shadow of the first.
- About one random patch in twelve is thin, late or sharp: a short Breath with a high Fade on Late or Hollow, or Reed and Whistle with Air and Effort near the top.
- Without vector instructions it costs about four to five per cent.
