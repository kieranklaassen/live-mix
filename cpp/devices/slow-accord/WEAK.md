# What is unproven in Slow Accord

Nobody has heard it. All of it was judged by figures.

- The slides. Three overtones at most move at once, each a pure tone sliding by
  up to several hundred cents. Metal learning, or a whistle? Listen to this
  first: the defaults, D3 A3 F4 C5 E5, held for five seconds.
- Swing and the middle of Bend move pitch in time and print under half a
  decibel a quarter. The low quarters of Attack, Ring, Release and Leftover and
  the tops of Forward and of Glow on slow pads are small steps. Rest is little
  heard where Again is low.
- Of random patches about one in eight is harsh or muddy: Stretched, Plate and
  Scattered with high Tilt, Touch or Order, and the bass goal on low keys.
- Plucked, Bar and Stretched are 2.5 dB apart, and so are Bell and Squeezed.
- A slow key struck again dips by 10 dB before it swells.
- Air at full leaves a soft hiss near 50 dB under the tone.
- The headers are long, about 1400 lines.

Then listen to Never resting pad, Cooling plate and Squeezed bass.
