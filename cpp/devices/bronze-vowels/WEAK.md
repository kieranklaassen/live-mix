# What is unproven in Bronze Vowels

Everything was measured and nothing was heard.

- Listen first to one held D3 at the defaults: does it say Ah, Oh, Oo, or only get darker? Formants, envelopes and spectra say the vowels are there up to C5. Ee is the weakest on keys under D3.
- Then D3 A3 F4 C5 E5 as a slow arpeggio with Contagion at 0, 1 and -1. Up, a strike rings the held bells awake in its vowel; down changes the print most. On keys struck together, up does little.
- On Bar with a short Ring the ten words come close (We and Hum 2 dB): wood is gone before they are said.
- With a Swell of half a second the mallets come close (Felt and Wood 1.3 dB): there is no strike left to tell them by.
- Nail on Plate with Tract up is harsh; about one random patch in twenty is.
- Ten keys at full gain go a little over the knee (0.64 at most) in one random patch in six. No own preset does.
- The first quarter of Swell and of Knock hardly moves the print at the defaults; the first 50 ms do change.
- Hush and Whoosh are air as much as bell.
